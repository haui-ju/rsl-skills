• Smart homes promise autonomy for people with Intellectual Disability (ID), but configuration remains challenging.
• First empirical study on ChatGPT-4o interpreting home automation for users with ID.
• 3-step clarification framework explored with 5 educators and 4 user-caregiver dyads.
• 12 design implications for accessible LLM-based EUD interfaces for users with ID.
