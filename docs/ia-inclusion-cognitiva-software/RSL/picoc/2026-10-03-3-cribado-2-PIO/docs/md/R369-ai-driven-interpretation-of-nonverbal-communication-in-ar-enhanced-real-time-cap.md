7     Conclusion
This dissertation proposes an AI-driven captioning ecosystem that
reimagines accessibility not as a matter of static compliance, but as
a space for dynamic, creative expression. By integrating non-verbal
cues—facial expressions, gestures, and vocal tone—into real-time
AR captions, the system supports deeper comprehension, reduced
cognitive load, and more engaging learning experiences for DHH
and neurodiverse students.
   Study 1 demonstrated that emotion-enhanced captions signifi-
cantly improve comprehension and reduce mental effort, affirming
the cognitive benefits of multimodal signaling. Study 2 will build
on these findings by investigating cultural variability in cue in-
terpretation, establishing design principles for culturally adaptive
systems. Study 3 will culminate in the development and evaluation
of a real-time AR prototype that operationalizes these insights in
inclusive classroom settings.
