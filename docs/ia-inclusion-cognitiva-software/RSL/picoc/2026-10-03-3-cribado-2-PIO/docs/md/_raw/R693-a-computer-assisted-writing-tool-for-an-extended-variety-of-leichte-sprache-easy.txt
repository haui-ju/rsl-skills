                                          Leichte Sprache (LS; easy-to-read German) deﬁnes a variety of German characterized by
                                          simpliﬁed syntactic constructions and a small vocabulary. It provides barrier-free
                                          information for a wide spectrum of people with cognitive impairments, learning
                                          difﬁculties, and/or a low level of literacy in the German language. The levels of difﬁculty
                                          of a range of syntactic constructions were systematically evaluated with LS readers as part
                                          of the recent LeiSA project (Bock, 2019). That study identiﬁed a number of constructions
                                          that were evaluated as being easy to comprehend but which fell beyond the deﬁnition of
                                          LS. We therefore want to broaden the scope of LS to include further constructions that LS
                                          readers can easily manage and that they might ﬁnd useful for putting their thoughts into
                                          words. For constructions not considered in the LeiSA study, we performed a comparative
                                          treebank study of constructions attested to in a collection of 245 LS documents from a
