   Upon completing a typing exercise, the keyboard makes a wave
motion, with each letter group providing feedback sequentially.
The exuberant feedback emphasizes and rewards the achievement,
following the component ‘game characteristics’ of the framework
of juicy design of games [12]. The wave is delivered through ei-
ther vibration or movement depending on which modality is active,
with movement preferred when both are active. Finally, texture
is added to the eight keys of the starting position. This tactile en-
hancement aids children in easily locating and keeping their fingers
on these keys while typing, facilitating better finger placement and
consistency.
